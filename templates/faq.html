Frequently Asked Questions

How much does profile unlocking cost?

Just ₹{{ unlock_price }} per profile — a one-time fee to view that specific profile's full photo and contact details. There is no membership or subscription.

How do I pay?

Scan the UPI QR code shown on the unlock page, or pay to our UPI ID directly, then upload your payment screenshot along with your name and mobile number.

Where do I send payment proof?

You can upload it directly on the unlock page, or send it to us on WhatsApp at {{ business_phone }} if that's easier for you.

How long does verification take?

Our team manually checks each payment, usually within a few hours. You'll be able to see the status under "My Requests" once you verify your number with an OTP.

Why are photos blurred?

To protect everyone's privacy. Full-resolution photos are only released to visitors whose payment has been verified for that specific profile.

Can I view a profile after payment?

Yes — once our team confirms your payment, the profile is marked "Unlocked" in "My Requests" and you can view the full photo and permitted contact details.

Is my phone number public?

No. Your number is only used to match your request and to send you an OTP when you want to check "My Requests". It is never shown publicly.

Can I request multiple profiles?

Yes. Each profile is unlocked separately for ₹{{ unlock_price }}, and all your requests will show up together under "My Requests".

What happens if my payment cannot be verified?

Your request will be marked "Rejected" and you'll be able to see that status. Please contact us on WhatsApp if you believe this was a mistake.

How do I contact support?

Call or WhatsApp us at {{ business_phone }}{% if business_email %}, or email {{ business_email }}{% endif %}.
